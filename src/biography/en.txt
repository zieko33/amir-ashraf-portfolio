Amir Ashraf
Amir Ashraf, known as “The Little Inventor,” is an Egyptian inventor born in Greater Cairo. He is currently studying in the Faculty of Engineering, specializing in mechatronics. He became known for innovations and inventions at an early age, received numerous national awards and awards in scientific and technological competitions, and was featured in several prominent Egyptian newspapers.

Early life and education
Amir’s passion for innovation began early. He received his first awards in sixth grade, winning a gold medal in the “Young Inventor” competition for a scientific invention model of his own design: Zieko Smart Home.
This win marked the beginning of a journey filled with achievements.

Projects and inventions
Amir has filed more than four patent applications and continues developing new ideas in technology and engineering. His principal projects include:

Zieko Vest (the air-conditioning shirt)
A shirt worn over underwear to maintain body temperature, controlled through an application on the wearer’s mobile phone.

First: in cold conditions
The shirt is worn to keep the body warm, maintaining its normal temperature or the temperature suited to the individual.
The intended experience is to feel as though it is spring and to move easily without wearing many layers of clothing that restrict movement and prevent certain tasks.

Second: in extreme heat
Zieko Vest is intended to make the wearer feel as though it is always spring, as if sitting in an air-conditioned room, without sweating, exhaustion, or loss of concentration. The goal is to make work easier in hot weather and demanding conditions, such as the desert or a workplace.

Running car air conditioning without running the engine
A new type of air conditioning designed to operate without Freon or starting the car engine. The invention is described as saving substantial fuel and avoiding consumption of engine power, unlike conventional air conditioning, which consumes fuel and power from the engine.

Multiple ideas for developing smart cars.
Multiple ideas for developing internal-combustion engines, aiming to increase efficiency to more than 70%; the biography states that internal-combustion engines have not exceeded 40% efficiency.

Future ambitions
Amir is looking for partnerships with businesspeople or factories to implement his innovations and turn them into actual products, especially in automotive technology and smart cooling. He also hopes to help other creative students. A scientific conference was previously organized for him to teach research methods to gifted students.

Life story
Amir began innovating at a very young age. At three years old, he attracted his mother’s attention when he took the hands from a broken wall clock to make a windscreen wiper for his toy car.
He had noticed that his toy car had no windscreen wipers.
He then began taking toys apart to discover their components. His mother helped him use a small screwdriver to dismantle toys and learn how each one worked. He started repairing his own toys, then those of friends and neighbors, until his neighbors knew him as “the little engineer” who repaired broken things.
At school, he became known as “the inventor” after entering his first competition in sixth grade and winning first prize and a gold medal.
